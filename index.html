Works With Airplane Mode ON:

When you open this app in Google Chrome on Android (or Safari on iPhone) and add it to your Home Screen, your phone installs it as a standalone app with all 32+ ammo loads and custom profiles saved locally on your device.

  1. 1
    Open the App in Your Phone's Browser Open this page in Google Chrome on your Android phone (or Safari on iOS).
  2. 2
    Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install App" Tap the three dots menu (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen" (on iPhone, tap the Share button at the bottom and tap "Add to Home Screen").
  3. 3
    Launch Anytime from Your Home Screen! A custom crosshair app icon named Ballistics will appear on your phone's home screen. Open it anytime—even with zero cell service in the woods or at the range!
